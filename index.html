PoseLab Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 10, 2026 · Contact: ozoone0923@gmail.com

PoseLab ("the app") is a posing-practice mirror for physique athletes. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: PoseLab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Camera

The front camera is used only as a live practice mirror on your screen. Camera frames are rendered in real time on your device and are never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. The camera permission is requested solely for this on-screen preview.

Data we collect

None. The app creates no accounts, uses no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking. Practice settings (division, level, timer target) are kept only in memory on your device.

Voice coaching

Coach cues are generated on-device using the operating system's text-to-speech engine. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

YouTube routines

If you paste a YouTube link, the video plays inside YouTube's official embedded player. That player is operated by Google and is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. PoseLab does not send your viewing activity anywhere.

Sharing

The "share report" action composes a text summary of your practice scores and opens the system share sheet. Sharing happens only when you choose it and only through apps you pick.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
